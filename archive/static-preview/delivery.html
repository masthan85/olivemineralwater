Delivery & Help

From our warehouse to your door

OLIVE delivers across Greater London & the Home Counties. Get in touch to discuss your address, preferred date, and order size.

hello@olivemineralwater.co.uk

How do I request an order?

Use the order form to tell us what you need. Send the email draft and wait for the team to confirm availability and delivery.

Can I track my delivery?

Use Track an Order to try the delivery status preview. For an actual delivery enquiry, email the OLIVE team with your order reference.

How do I set up a trade account?

Send a trade enquiry with your business details and expected volumes so the team can discuss pricing and delivery.

What if something is missing or damaged?

Email us with the order reference, the affected product and quantity, and the lot code if visible. Keep the packaging so the team can identify the item.
