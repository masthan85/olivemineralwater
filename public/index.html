Our Water

Drawn from nature, bottled with care

OLIVE Pure Mineral Water begins its journey high in protected uplands, where rainwater filters slowly through ancient rock, gathering its distinctive balance of natural minerals along the way.

We bottle it still and untouched — nothing added, nothing taken away — so every bottle pours exactly as nature intended. From a 250ml table bottle to a 1 litre carafe companion, there is an OLIVE for every occasion.
